# LifeOS AI

## Overview

LifeOS AI is an AI-powered personal productivity and accountability assistant designed to help users build consistency, organize their daily lives, and achieve their goals.

The application helps users plan their day, track habits, manage projects, and receive AI-powered recommendations based on their progress.

---

## Problem

Many people struggle with consistency. They start projects, create goals, and make plans but fail to stay accountable.

LifeOS AI aims to solve this problem by acting as a personal AI accountability partner.

---

## Goals

- Help users organize their daily schedule
- Track tasks and goals
- Monitor consistency
- Provide AI-powered suggestions
- Automate reminders and reports

---

## Features (Version 1)
